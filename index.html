01 — About

About me

I'm Musa Hameed, a student interested in computer science, software engineering, artificial intelligence, technology, and creative work. I enjoy learning across different fields and, most importantly, turning what I learn into something tangible.

A lot of my learning happens through projects. I experiment with software, electronics, embedded systems, product ideas, filmmaking, and digital content. Some projects become working builds; others are experiments that help me understand what is possible.

I'm currently working toward a future in software engineering with a particular interest in AI. At the same time, I'm developing the creative and communication skills that I believe make technology more useful: storytelling, filmmaking, public speaking, and understanding people.

02 — Work & Projects

Things I'm building & exploring

  • In Progress · Software · Fitness Technology

    Fitness Technology Platform

    I'm developing the concept and data structure for a fitness application focused on turning activity and health-related measurements into useful progress information. The planned experience includes activity sessions, calories, sleep, heart rate, pace, elevation, daily goals, and progress trends. I'm using FlutterFlow to explore the application's interface and database structure.

  • In Progress · Embedded Systems · Electronics

    Voice-Controlled Smart Lamp

    An experimental smart-lamp project built around an ESP32. I'm exploring how voice input, audio output, electronics, sensors, and physical devices can work together to create a small local assistant. The project is an ongoing exploration of embedded systems and the connection between software and hardware.

  • Electronics · Morse Code · Personal Project

    Telegraph Projects

    A series of experiments inspired by the original telegraph and Morse communication. These projects combine physical components with the logic of Morse code and are part of my interest in building unusual, hands-on technology rather than learning software only through a screen.

  • Electronics · 3D Printing · Experimentation

    Interactive Electronics Builds

    I've also experimented with physical technology projects, including a 3D-printed arc-reactor-inspired lamp and lighting concepts. These builds give me a practical way to learn about LEDs, electronics, physical design, and integrating hardware into an object.

  • Creative Work · Filmmaking · Storytelling

    Cinematic Filmmaking & Storytelling

    I'm interested in filmmaking as a form of storytelling, particularly cinematic historical and cultural content. I enjoy developing concepts, visual atmosphere, practical props, camera work, editing, and narrative structure rather than treating video simply as a way to deliver information.

  • Ongoing · YouTube · Digital Media

    History & Documentary Content

    I'm building ideas for a history-focused content channel that combines documentary-style research with cinematic presentation. The goal is to make subjects feel like stories rather than conventional lectures, using visuals, props, atmosphere, and editing to create a stronger viewing experience.

  • Entrepreneurship · Sports · Product Ideas

    Ace Allure

    Ace Allure was one of my early ventures into sports goods and entrepreneurship. The experience introduced me to product thinking, branding, selling, and the process of taking an idea beyond the planning stage. It also influenced my later interest in combining sports products with technology and software.

  • Initiative · SDG 4 · Education

    Education & Technology

    I'm interested in how technology can improve access to learning and make education more effective. My work around Sustainable Development Goal 4 — Quality Education — reflects an interest in using technology not just as an engineering tool, but as a way to solve real educational problems.

  • Achievement · Duke of Edinburgh's International Award

    Bronze Award

    Completed the Bronze level of the Duke of Edinburgh's International Award, an experience built around commitment, personal development, activity, service, and following through on goals over time.
